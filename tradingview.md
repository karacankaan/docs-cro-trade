# TradingView

cro.trade runs on [TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/) charts. Trusted by millions of traders as the top charting platform, TradingView can help you dive into [BTCUSD charts](https://www.tradingview.com/symbols/BTCUSD/) analysis, follow the [crypto](https://www.tradingview.com/crypto-screener/) universe, and break down price moves with custom indicators and additional tools.
