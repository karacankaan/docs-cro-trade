# Rewards & Fee Sharing

cro.trade shares its protocol trading fees with **CRONUS** holders. The **Rewards** page is where you track and claim your share, and manage your referral earnings.

Open it from your **Profile**, or the Rewards entry in the app.

## Two Tabs

* **Fees** — your share of protocol trading fees, paid in CRONUS
* **Referral** — your referral link and earnings — see [Referrals](referrals.md)

## Protocol Fees

The Fees tab shows your distribution from cro.trade's trading fees:

* **Claimable now** — the amount you can claim right now, shown in USD with the equivalent in CRONUS
* **Total received** — everything you've earned to date
* **Total claimed** — everything you've already claimed

Distributions are paid in **CRONUS** and are tied to your CRONUS holdings.

## Claiming

1. Tap **Claim**
2. The first time, accept the **Terms of Use** (read and check the box, then continue)
3. Confirm — your claim is processed on-chain and the claimed CRONUS is credited to you

Once claimed, your claimable balance resets and your **Total claimed** updates.

## 100% Fee-Funded

> **Transparent.** No tokens are minted or inflated. All distributions come exclusively from protocol trading fees via on-chain buybacks. Every transaction is publicly verifiable on the Cronos blockchain.

## Share Your Earnings

Tap **Share** to generate a shareable card showing your total earned, claimable, and total claimed — with your avatar and a link back to cro.trade.

## How Fees Are Generated

Spot trades on cro.trade carry a small fee (see [Fees](../reference/fees.md)). A portion funds the buybacks that back these CRONUS distributions, so the more the platform trades, the larger the pool shared with holders.
