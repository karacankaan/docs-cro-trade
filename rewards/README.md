# Rewards & Fee Sharing

cro.trade hands a share of its protocol trading fees to **CRONUS** holders. The **Rewards** page, reachable from your Profile, is where you track and claim it — and where your referral earnings live.

Two tabs: **Fees**, below, and **Referral**, covered in [Referrals](referrals.md).

## Fees

Three numbers. **Claimable now** is what you can take today, shown in USD with the CRONUS equivalent. **Total received** is everything you've earned since you started. **Total claimed** is what you've already taken.

Distributions are paid in **CRONUS** and scale with your CRONUS holdings. Every trade on the platform carries a fee ([Fees](../reference/fees.md) has the rates), so the pool grows with platform volume.

## Claiming

Tap **Claim**. The first time, you'll be asked to read and accept the Terms of Use — check the box and continue. Confirm, and the claim settles on-chain and credits you the CRONUS.

Your claimable balance resets to zero and **Total claimed** moves up by the amount.

## Sharing

**Share** builds a card with your total earned, claimable and total claimed, your avatar, and a link back to cro.trade.
