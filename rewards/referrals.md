# Referrals

You take **30%** of the trading fees the people you refer generate. It's in the **Referral** tab of the [Rewards](README.md) page, with your rate shown at the top.

A handful of accounts have a negotiated rate instead of the standard one; those carry a **VIP** badge next to the figure.

## Your link

It's built from your username:

```
https://cro.trade/?ref=yourname
```

**Copy** puts it on your clipboard.

## How someone becomes yours

Either they open your link — the code is remembered and applied the moment they connect a wallet — or they type it into the **Add a referral code** field during sign-up.

The tie is made once, when they first connect. You can't refer yourself.

## What you see

Two headline numbers, **Friends referred** and **Total earned** in USD, over two sub-tabs.

**Referred Users** lists each referred wallet, when they joined and what they've earned you. **Payments** lists your payout batches by date and amount; tap one for the detail, including how many referees it covered. Payouts are in **USDC**.

## Sharing

**Share Referral Card** builds an invite card carrying your code and a QR that goes straight to your referral URL.

Your code travels on the app's other share cards too — a position card, a P&L card — so someone joining from any of them is credited to you.
