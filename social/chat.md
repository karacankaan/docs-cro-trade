# Chat

Live public rooms, and the way you reach support.

On mobile, the **cro.trade logo** in the centre of the bottom bar opens it. On desktop, **ALPHA** in the header slides the rooms over whatever page you were on, so you can read them while watching a chart.

## Rooms

The rail down the left lists what you can open:

- **cro.trade | alpha** — the [trade feed](alpha.md) and the alphas posted against it, rather than a conversation
- **Cronos** — the chain in general
- **BANGER CHAT** — shared with banger.meme: messages appear in both
- **Token rooms** — a single token's community, CRONUS and NOOGLE among them
- **Support** — your own private thread with the team

Public rooms have their own links, so you can point someone at one directly: `cro.trade/chat/noogle`.

## Reading and posting

Reading is open to anyone, signed in or not.

Posting needs a balance, and which balance is the room's business. The app's own rooms ask for a dollar amount that **CRO or USDC** can satisfy. The **Cronos** room asks for that amount specifically in **CRO**. A **token room** asks for a holding of its own token.

When you can't post yet, the bar above the keyboard names the threshold for that room and what you're currently holding, with a **Re-check** button for after you've topped up.

## Writing

Text, emoji, GIFs, images, and replies that quote the message above yours. Typing `@` brings up username suggestions.

Three things expand on their own: a **token address** becomes a card with the live price, a **link to a post on X** becomes the post itself, and any other link becomes a compact chip. You can react to messages, and you can see who's typing.

## Alerts in the room

Some messages come from the platform rather than a person. **Buy alerts** mark a notable buy of the room's token, carrying the buyer's face and the size. **Launch alerts** announce a new launchpad token from someone in the room, with a live price card.

## Mentions

**Settings → Notifications → Chat Mentions** pushes a notification when someone @mentions you in a public room. See [Notifications](../account/notifications.md).

## Moderation

**Block** a trader and their messages stop reaching you. **Report** sends a message to the moderators.

Moderators can mute an account. A mute isn't a balance problem, so topping up won't lift it.

## Support

Tickets are opened from inside Chat — **Support** in the rail, or **Contact support** wherever it appears in the app. The form asks for your name and wallet address (filled in when you're signed in), a subject (Swap Issue, Account, Bug Report, Suggestion, Other) and what went wrong.

The ticket becomes a private thread in your rail. Replies land there, and it stays put so you can follow up later.
