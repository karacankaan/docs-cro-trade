# Chat

**Chat** is cro.trade's live public chat. It's where traders talk about what's moving, and it's also where you reach support.

## Opening Chat

* **On mobile** — tap the **cro.trade logo** in the centre of the bottom navigation bar
* **On desktop** — tap **ALPHA** in the header, and the rooms slide in over whatever page you're on, so you can keep watching a chart while you read

## Rooms

The rail down the left edge lists the rooms you can open:

* **cro.trade | alpha** — the live [Feed](feed.md) of trade activity and the alphas people post on it, rather than a conversation
* **Cronos** — general talk about the chain
* **BANGER CHAT** — shared with banger.meme; messages sent here land in that room too, and vice versa
* **Token rooms** — a room for a specific token's community, such as CRONUS or NOOGLE
* **Support** — your own private thread with the team (see below)

Every public room has its own link, so you can share one directly — for example `cro.trade/chat/noogle`.

## Reading and Posting

**Reading is open to everyone.** You don't need to sign in to follow a public room.

**Posting requires holding a balance**, and the amount depends on the room:

* The app's own rooms ask for a dollar amount, which your **CRO or USDC** can cover
* The **Cronos** room asks for a holding in **CRO** specifically
* A **token room** asks for a holding of **that token**

If you can't post yet, the bar above the keyboard tells you the exact threshold for that room and what you're currently holding, with a **Re-check** button for after you top up.

## Writing a Message

The composer supports:

* **Text and emoji**
* **GIFs** — search and pick from the GIF panel
* **Images**
* **Replies** — reply to a message and it's quoted above yours
* **@mentions** — start typing `@` and pick from the username suggestions

Some things expand into a card automatically:

* **A token address** becomes a token card with its live price
* **A link to a post on X** becomes an embedded post you can tap to enlarge
* **Other links** become a compact link chip

You can also react to a message, and you'll see when other people in the room are typing.

## Alerts in the Room

Some messages are posted by the platform rather than by a person:

* **Buy alerts** — a notable buy on the room's token, shown with the buyer's avatar and the size
* **Launch alerts** — a new launchpad token from someone in the room, with a live price card

## Mentions & Notifications

Turn on **Chat Mentions** in **Settings → Notifications** to get a push notification when someone @mentions you in a public room. See [Notifications](../account/notifications.md).

## Moderation

* **Block** — block a trader and you stop seeing their messages
* **Report** — report a message to the moderators
* Moderators can mute an account; a muted account can't post until the mute is lifted, and topping up a balance won't lift it

## Support

Support tickets are opened from inside Chat. Tap **Support** in the rail (or **Contact support** wherever it appears in the app) and fill in the short form:

1. Your **name** and **wallet address** (pre-filled when you're signed in)
2. A **subject** — Swap Issue, Account, Bug Report, Suggestion, or Other
3. Describe the problem

Your ticket becomes a private thread in your rail. Replies from the team arrive there, and the thread stays available so you can follow up.
