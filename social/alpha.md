# cro.trade | alpha

**alpha** is the trade feed: what everyone on the platform is buying and selling, and what they have to say about it.

It sits inside [Chat](chat.md) as the first room in the rail. On desktop, the **ALPHA** button in the header opens it over whatever page you were on. Reading it needs no account.

## What's on it

Spot trades on Cronos, perps trades, and the alphas people attach to their positions, newest first. Market signals and news are mixed in.

Two filters narrow it. **All / Following** switches between everyone and the traders you follow. The venue filter cuts the list to **Cronos**, **Perps** or **Predictions**.

## Posting an alpha

An alpha is a comment attached to a position you hold, not a free-standing post.

From alpha, tap the **+** in the corner and pick the position you want to talk about; your open positions are listed largest first. Starting from a position you're already looking at — the trade sheet, a token page, Explore — skips that step and opens the composer directly.

You get 200 characters. Posting needs a connected wallet, and links are restricted.

## On each row

Like it, bookmark it, or share it: sharing copies a block-explorer link for a spot trade and a summary for a perp. Tapping the row opens the trade in full; tapping the trader opens their [profile](../features/profiles.md), where you can follow them and move them into your Following filter.
