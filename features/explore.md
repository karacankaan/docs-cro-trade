# Explore

Explore is the market list. Three sections sit behind the tabs at the top — **Discover**, your **Watchlist**, and **Perps** — and everything on the page streams live, so prices, volumes and rankings move without a refresh.

## Discover

Three lists, switched by the sub-tabs.

**Hot** ranks tokens by recent trading activity across Cronos DEXes. **Gainers** ranks the same universe by price change instead. Both follow the timeframe you pick — 5m, 1h, 6h or 24h — which changes the volume and change figures, not which tokens appear.

**Launchpad** covers newly launched tokens, grouped **New**, **Pre-Graduate** and **Graduated**. Those cards carry the platform badge, a progress bar where there's a bonding curve to fill, and a **TAX** marker on fee-on-transfer tokens. [Launchpad](launchpad.md) has the detail.

## Token cards

A card carries the logo, name and symbol; the price in USD and its change over the selected timeframe; volume, market cap and liquidity; and the token's age. The bar underneath splits buy against sell volume, or shows graduation progress on a curve token.

Tap the card to open the token's [trade page](trade.md), or the star to add it to your watchlist. Social links and a copy-address button sit on the card itself.

## Watchlist

The tokens you've starred. It's tied to your account rather than the device, so it follows you between sessions and devices once you're signed in.

## Perps

A short list of [perpetual futures](../perps/README.md) markets, each card showing the funding rate, open interest, 24h volume and a price sparkline. Tap one to open its trading page.
