# Search

The search icon in the header opens search from anywhere; there's a full page for it too. Results appear as you type — nothing to submit.

Three kinds of thing come back, ranked together by relevance and liquidity, so heavily traded tokens surface first.

**Tokens** match on name, symbol, or a pasted contract address, and show price and liquidity. Tap one for its [trade page](trade.md).

**Traders** match on a `0x…` address, a `.cro` name, or a registered username. Tap one for their [profile](profiles.md).

**Perps markets** match on symbol — BTC, ETH — and go straight to that market's [trading page](../perps/README.md).
