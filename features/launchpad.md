# Launchpad

The Launchpad tracks newly launched tokens that are still on their bonding curve — before they graduate to a full DEX listing. You can find it as a tab under **Explore → Discover → Launchpad**.

## What is a Bonding Curve?

When a token launches on a bonding-curve platform:

* The price increases as more people buy
* There's a set target that needs to be raised
* Once the target is reached, the token **graduates** — liquidity is added to a DEX and open trading begins

## Supported Platforms

cro.trade tracks launches from four platforms on Cronos, each with its own badge and progress-bar color:

* **CroFun** — Cronos-native launchpad
* **AgentFun** — AI-agent-themed launches
* **WolfStreet** — supports fee-on-transfer (tax) tokens
* **PuushFun** — bonding-curve launches

Each platform sets its own base token and graduation target. The progress shown on each card reflects how close the token is to its specific target.

## Token Stages

Launchpad tokens move through three stages:

1. **New** — freshly created, early on the curve. Cards show volume, market cap, and graduation progress.
2. **Pre-Graduate** — meaningful progress made toward graduation, still on the curve.
3. **Graduated** — reached the target and moved to a DEX. Cards now show liquidity, volume, and market cap (no curve bar).

## Launchpad Card

Each card shows:

* Token name, symbol, and logo
* **Platform badge** (CroFun, AgentFun, WolfStreet, or PuushFun)
* **Progress bar** toward graduation (for pre-graduation tokens)
* Volume, market cap, and price
* A **TAX** indicator if the token charges a fee-on-transfer tax

## Trading Launchpad Tokens

You can buy and sell launchpad tokens directly on cro.trade:

1. Tap a token card to open its trade page
2. Use the Buy/Sell interface — the same flow as a regular [Trade](trade.md)
3. Pre-graduation trades execute against the bonding curve; graduated tokens route through the DEX aggregator

## Creating a Token

On supported platforms you can launch your own token directly from cro.trade. Look for the **Create Token** option on the Launchpad (CroFun creation flow). Follow the prompts to set up your token and start its bonding curve.

## Graduation

When a token reaches its funding target:

* Liquidity is automatically added to a DEX
* The token becomes available for regular trading
* It moves into the Graduated stage and the main Explore feed
