# Connect Your Wallet

Two ways in, and both end with a self-custodial wallet whose private key you can export.

## Bringing your own wallet

Tap **Connect** in the header and pick one:

| Wallet | Type |
| --- | --- |
| **Crypto.com Wallet** | Mobile / browser extension |
| **MetaMask** | Browser extension / mobile |
| **WalletConnect** | QR code, for mobile wallets |

Any other browser wallet you already have installed — Rabby, for instance — is detected and listed alongside them. Approve the connection and your address appears in the header.

## Signing in without one

If you don't have a crypto wallet, sign in with **email** (a one-time code), **Google**, **Apple**, **Telegram**, or a **passkey** (Face ID, Touch ID, or a security key). A wallet is created for you as part of signing in, and you can export its private key whenever you want.

## Linking sign-in methods

Email, Google, Apple and Telegram can all point at the same account, so it doesn't matter which one you reach for. Manage them under **Settings → Linked Accounts**.

## More than one wallet

An account holds up to **20 wallets**. Create new ones, import existing ones from a private key, and switch the active wallet at any time — see [Account & Wallets](../account/wallets.md).

## Networks

Spot trading runs on **Cronos mainnet** (chain ID 25); the app prompts you to switch if you're on something else.

Perps and Predict settle in **USDC** and are funded in-app, so there's no network to switch by hand.

## Disconnecting

Tap your wallet in the header, or go to **Settings → Wallets**, and choose **Disconnect** — or just switch to another wallet.
